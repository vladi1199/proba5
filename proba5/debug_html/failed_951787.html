Frequently Asked Questions

What is OptinMonster?


OptinMonster is a lead generation and conversion optimization platform that helps you convert website visitors into subscribers and customers using popups, floating bars, and targeted campaigns.

Do I need to have coding skills to use OptinMonster?

Absolutely not. You can create and customize beautiful popups, floating bars, fullscreens, slide ins, and more from our large template library without any coding knowledge. We made it extremely user friendly, so you can build and A/B test high-converting lead-capture forms without hiring a developer.

What’s websites does OptinMonster work with?

OptinMonster can be installed on WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, and nearly every website platform on the internet. Plus, we integrate with every major email marketing service so your lead gen never gets locked in to one service. As long as you can add our embed code to your website like you would a Facebook or Google Analytics script, you’ll be fine. With OptinMonster, you can create WordPress popups, Shopify popups, Wix popups, Sqaurespace popups and… you get the idea. It just works.

How much does OptinMonster cost?

An OptinMonster subscription starts as low as $7/month. We offer both monthly and annual plans, along with significant discounts for first-time customers to help you hit the ground running. And if you’re using our WordPress popup plugin, you can even show your campaigns 500 times for FREE! It’s a great way to get started quickly, with no risk.

What integrations are supported?

OptinMonster connects natively with all major email marketing platforms and CRMs, but we also provide powerful workarounds for more niche setups. You can easily bridge any gap using Zapier, Webhooks, or Uncanny Automator, and our robust Public API is available for developers building custom, enterprise-grade connections. No matter your tech stack, we ensure your lead data flows seamlessly.

Will this work for my site?

If you have traffic and use email, this is built for you.

If your website gets steady traffic and you already use email marketing, OptinMonster gives you more chances to convert the visitors you’re currently losing.
